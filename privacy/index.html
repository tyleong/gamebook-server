Gamebook Adventures

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 July 2026 · Version 1.1
Gamebook Adventures titles are interactive fiction games published for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights. It applies to Ember Crown, The Skyborn, 霜华剑歌 / SwordSong, Mia, and future titles in the Gamebook Adventures series published by the same developer.
📋

1. Information We Collect

Gamebook Adventures titles are text-based games with no account, login, or profile required. We do not ask for your name, date of birth, email address, or any personal details to play.

The only data collected is through third-party services embedded in some versions of the app:

Service Data Collected Purpose Shared With
Google AdMob Advertising ID, device information, IP address, and ad interaction data Show ads in the free version Google
Google Play Billing Purchase receipt, product ID, and purchase status Verify in-app purchases such as Remove Ads Google
Google Play Console Crash reports, diagnostics, device type, Android version, and app version App stability monitoring and bug fixing Google
Game progress, save slots, endings found, achievements, choices made, and ad-free state are stored on your device only using local app storage. They are not transmitted to us or any server.
🎮

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Ads - If you use a free version, Google AdMob may serve personalised or non-personalised ads based on your device and Google advertising settings.
  • In-app purchases - If you purchase Remove Ads or a similar upgrade, Google Play processes the transaction. We receive only the purchase status needed to unlock the feature. No payment card or banking details are visible to us.
  • Crash data - Google Play Console may collect crash reports and diagnostics to help us fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Local progress - The app stores game progress locally so you can continue your story, replay endings, and keep achievements or save slots.

We do not use any data for profiling, direct marketing, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

🔒

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for our own marketing purposes.

The third-party services involved are:

  • Google AdMob - advertising. Google's Privacy Policy applies.
  • Google Play - app distribution, purchases, billing, crash reporting, and diagnostics. Google's Privacy Policy applies.

We operate no backend server for Gamebook Adventures titles. No gameplay data is sent to us directly.

📅

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and app state stored on your device can be deleted at any time by:

  • Clearing the app's data: Settings - Apps - [game title] - Storage - Clear Data, or
  • Uninstalling the app.

We do not retain gameplay data on our own servers because we do not operate servers for these games.

Google Play purchase records, AdMob advertising data, crash reports, and diagnostics are handled by Google according to Google's policies and your Google account or device settings.

👶

5. Children's Privacy

Gamebook Adventures titles are intended for users aged 12 and above, unless a specific title's store listing states a different age rating. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through one of our apps, please contact us using the details below.

🛡

6. Security

  • Game progress and save data are stored locally on your device.
  • Ad serving, billing, crash reporting, and diagnostics use Google's infrastructure.
  • We do not operate servers that store your personal information for these games.
⚖

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it.

  • For data stored on your device, you can exercise these rights directly by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
  • For data held by Google, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy, Google Play, and your Google account settings.
📝

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date and version at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

This policy covers Gamebook Adventures titles published by the same developer, including Ember Crown, The Skyborn, 霜华剑歌 / SwordSong, Mia, and future titles with the same offline gamebook data model.
